Weekends and observed dates

Three holidays in 2027 fall on a weekend, so the weekday off moves under the observance rules in 5 U.S.C. 6103 and the guidance published by the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (Saturday to the Friday before, Sunday to the Monday after):

  • Juneteenth National Independence Day falls on Saturday, June 19, and is observed on Friday, June 18.
  • Independence Day falls on Sunday, July 4, and is observed on Monday, July 5.
  • Christmas Day falls on Saturday, December 25, and is observed on Friday, December 24.

New Year's Day 2028 falls on Saturday, January 1, so it is observed on Friday, December 31, 2027. That Friday is a holiday for the following year's New Year's Day, not an extra 2027 Christmas observance.

Inauguration Day is not a holiday in 2027. It is a holiday only for some federal employees in the Washington, D.C. area on January 20 of each fourth year after 1965, and 2027 is not one of those years.

These dates are computed from the statutory rules each time this page is generated, rather than copied from a one-year table. They match the 2027 schedule published by OPM. Laws can change; if Congress adds or moves a holiday, this page will need an update.
