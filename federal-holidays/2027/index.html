U.S. federal holidays in 2027

The eleven legal public holidays for 2027, with the date each one falls on and the day it is observed when that date is a Saturday or Sunday.

There are eleven U.S. federal holidays in 2027. Three fall on a weekend and are observed on a nearby weekday: Juneteenth on Friday, June 18; Independence Day on Monday, July 5; and Christmas Day on Friday, December 24. New Year’s Day 2028 is also observed in 2027, on Friday, December 31.

Weekends and observed dates

Three holidays in 2027 fall on a weekend, so the weekday off moves under the observance rules in 5 U.S.C. 6103 and the guidance published by the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (Saturday to the Friday before, Sunday to the Monday after):

  • Juneteenth National Independence Day falls on Saturday, June 19, and is observed on Friday, June 18.
  • Independence Day falls on Sunday, July 4, and is observed on Monday, July 5.
  • Christmas Day falls on Saturday, December 25, and is observed on Friday, December 24.

New Year's Day 2028 falls on Saturday, January 1, so it is observed on Friday, December 31, 2027. That Friday is a holiday for the following year's New Year's Day, not an extra 2027 Christmas observance.

Inauguration Day is not a holiday in 2027. It is a holiday only for some federal employees in the Washington, D.C. area on January 20 of each fourth year after 1965, and 2027 is not one of those years.

These dates are computed from the statutory rules each time this page is generated, rather than copied from a one-year table. They match the 2027 schedule published by OPM. Laws can change; if Congress adds or moves a holiday, this page will need an update.

Frequently asked questions

When is Juneteenth observed in 2027?

June 19, 2027 is a Saturday, so Juneteenth is observed on Friday, June 18, 2027.

When is the Fourth of July holiday in 2027?

July 4, 2027 is a Sunday, so Independence Day is observed on Monday, July 5, 2027.

When is Christmas observed in 2027?

December 25, 2027 is a Saturday, so Christmas Day is observed on Friday, December 24, 2027.

Is December 31, 2027 a federal holiday?

Yes, for New Year’s Day 2028. January 1, 2028 is a Saturday, so it is observed on Friday, December 31, 2027.

When are Thanksgiving, Memorial Day, and Labor Day in 2027?

Thanksgiving Day is Thursday, November 25; Memorial Day is Monday, May 31; and Labor Day is Monday, September 6, 2027.

How many business days are in 2027?

2027 has 261 weekdays. Leaving out the twelve weekday federal holiday observances that fall in 2027, including December 31 for New Year’s Day 2028, leaves 249 business days.
