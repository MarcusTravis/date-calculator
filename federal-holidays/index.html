U.S. federal holidays

Choose a year to see each federal holiday, the date it falls on, and the day it is observed.

There are eleven U.S. federal holidays, set by 5 U.S.C. 6103. Five have fixed dates and six fall on a set weekday. When a holiday lands on a Saturday, federal employees get the Friday before off; when it lands on a Sunday, they get the Monday after. Choose any year from 1971 on to see both dates.

How the dates are set

Federal holidays are set by law in 5 U.S.C. 6103. Five have fixed dates (New Year's Day, Juneteenth from 2021, Independence Day, Veterans Day, and Christmas Day) and the rest fall on a set weekday, such as the third Monday in January or the fourth Thursday in November.

When a fixed-date holiday lands on a Saturday, federal employees get the Friday before; on a Sunday, they get the Monday after. These are the observance rules described by the U.S. Office of Personnel Management. One consequence: when January 1 is a Saturday, the observed holiday is December 31 of the year before, which is why a year can sometimes show a New Year's holiday from the following year in its notes.

Inauguration Day, which is a holiday only for some federal employees in the Washington, D.C. area every four years, is not included.

Frequently asked questions

How many federal holidays are there?

Eleven since 2021, when Juneteenth National Independence Day was added. Before that there were ten, and before 1986 there were nine, since Martin Luther King Jr. Day was first observed in 1986.

What happens when a federal holiday falls on a weekend?

Following OPM’s rules for federal employees, a Saturday holiday is observed on the Friday before and a Sunday holiday on the Monday after.

Why is New Year’s Day sometimes observed in December?

When January 1 is a Saturday, the observed holiday is Friday, December 31 of the year before. That is why a year can list a New Year’s observance from the following year in its notes.

When is Juneteenth observed?

Juneteenth is June 19 and has been a federal holiday since 2021. When June 19 is a Saturday it is observed on Friday, June 18; when it is a Sunday, on Monday, June 20.

Are Christmas Eve and the day after Thanksgiving federal holidays?

No. They are not in the law. A president sometimes gives federal employees one of those days off by executive order, but such one-time days are not included here.

Is Inauguration Day a federal holiday?

Only for some federal employees in the Washington, D.C. area, on January 20 every four years, so it is not included in this list.
